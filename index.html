Vitanote Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 September 2026 · App: Vitanote (Android, package com.vitanote.app)

In short: Vitanote keeps everything you enter on your phone. We do not collect, upload, sell or share your personal or health information. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking.

1. Who we are

Vitanote is an Android app that helps you keep a blood pressure log, take medicines on time and follow an intermittent fasting plan. It is published by an independent developer ("we", "us"). Contact details are at the end of this policy.

2. Information you enter

You may enter:

  • blood pressure readings (systolic, diastolic, pulse), the time, notes and whether you had taken your blood pressure medicine;
  • medicine names, doses, instructions, schedules and whether each dose was taken or skipped;
  • fasting start and end times and your chosen fasting plan;
  • reminder times.

This information is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we have no way to see it.

3. Information we collect

None. Vitanote contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software, does not require an account and does not use the internet to send your data anywhere.

4. Purchases

Vitanote offers an optional one-time "Pro" upgrade sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under the Google Privacy Policy. We never receive your name, email address or payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether Pro has been purchased on your account so it can unlock Pro features.

5. Sharing reports

You can choose to export a PDF of your blood pressure, medication or fasting records. The file is created on your device and is shared only with the app or person you pick (for example, your doctor or a family member). We never receive a copy.

6. Permissions

PermissionWhy Vitanote uses it
NotificationsTo show your blood pressure, medication and fasting reminders.
Alarms & reminders (exact alarms)So medication reminders arrive at the exact minute. You can decline; reminders may then arrive a few minutes late.
Run at startupTo restore your reminders after your phone restarts.

Vitanote does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your records stay on your device until you delete them. You can delete individual readings, fasts and medicines in the app, or remove all data by uninstalling Vitanote or clearing its storage in your phone's settings. Because we hold no copy, we cannot restore deleted data, so export a PDF of anything you want to keep.

8. Security

Your data is protected by Android's app sandbox, which keeps other apps from reading it. We recommend using a screen lock on your phone.

9. Children

Vitanote is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

10. Medical disclaimer

Vitanote is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. It records the information you enter and does not measure blood pressure. Always consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If we ever start collecting any data, we will say so here and in the app's Google Play Data safety section before the change takes effect.
